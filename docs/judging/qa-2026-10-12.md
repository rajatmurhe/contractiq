## Model quality: preserve the failures

The fresh eight-case first pass found 80% recall and 66.7% precision. A separate six-case first pass completed only five requests. These exposed numeric misunderstandings, wrong topic coverage and altered quotation casing. All reports are retained in [evaluation evidence](../../evals/review/README.md#october-12-customer-workflow-qa).

After fixes, the 20-case numeric regression completed 20/20 with 100% rule precision/recall and missing-topic matching. A later six-case regression completed 6/6 with 100% risk-rule precision/recall but only 83.3% missing-topic exact match. Different revisions and corpora are identified in each report by source hash. Do not combine these into a general accuracy claim. Explanations and proposed wording still require human judgment. Provider prices are unconfigured, so cost is unknown.
