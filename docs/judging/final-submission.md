## Description for the submission form

contractIQ helps procurement teams turn supplier agreements into traceable negotiation decisions. Upload a searchable PDF, DOCX or text file, inspect the extracted text, and review five explicit commercial preferences. Each finding includes a source quotation and proposed wording. A dashboard assistant retrieves passages from the selected agreement, answers with clickable citations, and preserves a conversation for that review. Revision comparison shows changed text and changes in flagged risks. A reviewer records a reasoned decision and exports a printable report or JSON evidence bundle.

The product uses Next.js, TypeScript and FastAPI. Google sign-in and isolated guest access are supported. The landing assistant explains the product. AI responses are validated against source passages, uncertainty is shown separately from missing topics, and approval remains a user action. Early access is free; proposed plans are $49/month for 50 reviews and $199/month for 300 reviews, with enterprise pricing subject to requirements. Billing and willingness to pay have not been validated.

## Three-minute demonstration

1. Open the live product and show Google login or free guest entry. Explain that the separate sample is curated.
2. Upload `demo-original.txt`, inspect the extraction, and run live review. Open a finding and its source.
3. Open the dashboard assistant. Ask about liability, then ask a question about a clause not flagged. Click its source citation. Show review scope and coverage.
4. Upload `demo-revised.txt`. Show the liability change and newly introduced renewal issue. Ask what changed; inspect version labels.
5. Request changes with a reason. Download the report and JSON. Show the proposed pricing and briefly state the limits.

Use synthetic documents in the demo. A video recording has not been generated by this document; record the verified deployed flow if the submission form requires a video.

## Evidence and limits

See `qa-2026-10-13.md` for the latest verification state, and `../../evals/review/README.md` for earlier live measurements including failures. Source checks establish provenance, not correctness of interpretation. Retrieval is lexical passage retrieval with nearby context, not embedding search. The assistant validates citation membership, not semantic entailment. No independent legal-quality or ROI claim is made.

Free Render hosting remains enabled at the owner's request. It may sleep and reset SQLite data on deployment. Export important reviews; the public service is not a durable contract repository. Manual backup/restore tooling is included for hosts with filesystem access. User deletion removes active review, chat and audit records; external provider records and backups have separate retention. Guest expiry does not itself delete records. No organization roles, SSO, automated retention or regulated compliance certification is claimed.

The practitioner kit is ready but no participant is available. Google browser sign-in was previously confirmed by the account owner; latest release-specific verification must be recorded in the QA report. Codex assisted development and testing.
